RANKALA LAKE

Rankala Lake is one of the most famous tourist attractions in Kolhapur.

MAHALAKSHMI TEMPLE

Mahalakshmi Temple is one of the most important temples in Maharashtra.


SHALINI PALACE

Shalini Palace is a magnificent heritage palace located on the western bank of Rankala Lake in Kolhapur, Maharashtra. It is one of the city's most beautiful landmarks and is known for its royal architecture, historical significance, and scenic surroundings.

SHREE JOTIBA TEMPLE

Jyotiba Temple is one of the most famous pilgrimage sites in Maharashtra. It is located on Jyotiba Hill (Wadi Ratnagiri), about 18 km northwest of Kolhapur, at an elevation of around 3,124 feet above sea level.
